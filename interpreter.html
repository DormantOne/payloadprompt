About this experiment

Imagine a very small community whose members grew up speaking only Klingon. Someone needs an interpreter to speak with an English speaker about something deeply personal. The interpreter is also a member of that small community.

Even with a trustworthy interpreter who keeps everything confidential, the speaker may feel uncomfortable revealing something private to someone they might encounter again. Could an artificial interpreter someday offer another layer of social privacy? That is one question motivating this experiment.

An AI service has its own limitations. It may misunderstand speech, make mistakes, or retain information. This exercise does not establish suitability for real-world use.

Read this first

Here’s how to start your spoken conversation.

  1. In Copilot, click in the message box and paste with Ctrl + V (Windows), ⌘ + V (Mac), or touch and hold and select Paste.
  2. Send the message.
  3. Turn on voice chat using the waveform icon. Allow microphone access if asked. The dictation microphone alone is not voice chat.
  4. Take turns speaking.
