Design intent: this does not choose an order by itself. It builds a disciplined prompt that asks for best imaging choice, alternatives, contrast risk, mitigation, and local protocol confirmation.
Important: Do not enter patient identifiers. If urgent/unstable, use radiology/ED/stroke/vascular pathways rather than waiting for an AI answer. The prompt is copied to your clipboard — paste it into the OpenEvidence or Copilot chat box after the tab opens.
Known limitation: percentages are only requested “when available.” The prompt tells the model not to invent sensitivity/specificity or risk numbers.
